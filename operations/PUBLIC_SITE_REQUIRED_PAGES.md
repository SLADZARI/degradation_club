# Dementor Club — required public site pages

Status: approved structural baseline
Updated: 2026-08-23

This file defines the minimum public-page structure of Dementor Club. It does not approve payment credentials, legal entities, prices, contact addresses or membership mechanics by itself.

## Core public areas

- Home
- About / Club
- Events
- Projects
- Community
- Merch
- Join
- Archive

## Entity pages

Every approved event, project, course and public Dementor may have a stable addressable URL.

Current approved public Dementor roster is maintained in `people/dementors.md`.

## Utility public pages

The official site must also provide:

### Donate / Support

Purpose: a stable page where a visitor can support development of the club.

Current structural status: APPROVED PAGE / PAYMENT METHOD PENDING.

Until payment details are separately approved, the public page must not invent:
- bank/card/crypto/payment-provider credentials;
- legal entity or recipient name;
- suggested donation amounts;
- subscription promises;
- tax/deduction claims.

The page may truthfully state that support mechanics are being prepared and can later expose approved methods without changing the URL.

### Contacts

Purpose: a stable contact page for public enquiries.

Current structural status: APPROVED PAGE / PUBLIC CONTACT CHANNEL PENDING.

Until a channel is separately approved, the site must not invent an email address, phone number, social account or physical address.

### Privacy / Terms

These are implementation/legal pages of the official site. They should describe only actual site behaviour and approved rules. They must be updated before introducing server-side account storage, mailing lists, payment processing or other data collection.

## Rule

A missing content field is shown as pending or omitted. A structural page may exist before its operational mechanism is activated.
