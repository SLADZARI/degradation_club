# 5. Business-analysis closure test

Business analysis can be considered sufficient for the first commercialization cycle when we can answer, with evidence or explicitly labeled hypotheses:

1. **Who is the first market segment?**
2. **What exact Thing / experience creates initial value for that segment?**
3. **What observable behavior proves value?**
4. **How do we reach the segment?**
5. **What exact value object may be paid?**
6. **What does it cost us to produce and fulfill?**
7. **What price / margin / capacity range is viable?**
8. **Who owns delivery and editorial decisions?**
9. **What legal/commercial setup is required for the chosen geography?**
10. **What evidence permits the next investment step?**

Until then:

> **DO NOT CONFUSE A COMPLETE PRODUCT MODEL WITH A VALIDATED BUSINESS MODEL.**
