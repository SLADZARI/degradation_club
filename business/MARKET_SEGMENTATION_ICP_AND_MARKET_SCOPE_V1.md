# DEMENTOR CLUB — MARKET SEGMENTATION / ICP / MARKET SCOPE v1

Status: **DRAFT / VALIDATION AUTHORITY CANDIDATE**  
Updated: **2026-09-15**

## Purpose

Define the first market to validate without inventing a demographic persona unsupported by evidence.

The Product Package already defines audience intents and modes. This document adds a business layer:

```text
WHO IS MOST LIKELY TO VALUE THIS NOW?
→ HOW DO WE RECOGNIZE THEM?
→ WHAT FIRST THING / OFFER FITS THEM?
→ WHAT MARKET SCOPE ARE WE ACTUALLY TESTING?
```

---

# 1. Current evidence status

Current product evidence is insufficient to approve a demographic / firmographic ICP.

Therefore:

- age is not an ICP by itself;
- income is not an ICP by itself;
- profession is not an ICP by itself;
- city is not an ICP by itself;
- `member` is not an ICP;
- people who like satire are too broad to be an ICP.

Use **behavior + situation + willingness to continue** first.

# 2. Segment hypotheses v0

## S1 · Cultural / intellectual audience

### Job

> Show me something accurate and strange about normal life that I would not have formulated myself.

### Likely first value

- game;
- text;
- short interactive Thing;
- Project episode;
- Event premise.

### Potential monetization later

- live experience;
- paid digital Thing;
- physical Thing;
- recurring Program only after repeat value.

### Strong fit signals

- completes the Thing;
- voluntarily opens another Thing;
- shares it privately;
- returns without a discount / notification debt.

---

## S2 · Creative / knowledge worker

### Job

> Give me a sharper way to see a familiar problem, habit or professional absurdity.

### Likely first value

- interactive Course;
- Tool;
- Method;
- Practice;
- Situation-based Thing.

### Potential monetization

- Tool / Method;
- Course;
- facilitated Practice;
- Intervention.

### Strong fit signals

- repeat use;
- Situation → resource fit;
- explicit interest in a live format;
- willingness to pay for a concrete outcome/experience.

---

## S3 · Founder / manager / consultant / team lead

### Job

> Break premature clarity around a real situation and help us see a better frame.

### Likely first value

- Situation-based Method / Tool;
- small facilitated session;
- Event / Intervention proof.

### Potential monetization

- high-touch Intervention;
- team format;
- B2B-paid experience where payer may differ from user.

### Strong fit signals

- brings a concrete Situation;
- accepts a paid diagnostic/facilitated action;
- reports/usefully demonstrates an outcome;
- referral / repeat.

# 3. First-market decision rule

Do not pick the first market by perceived prestige.

Score each segment on:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Value fit | Does an existing Thing already solve/provide the job? |
| Reachability | Can we reach 30–100 plausible people without large paid media? |
| Experience clarity | Can they understand and experience value without learning the ontology? |
| Continuation | Is there a truthful next Thing / next action? |
| Commercial adjacency | Is there a concrete paid object that could logically follow later? |
| Fulfillment feasibility | Can the team deliver it now? |
| Legal/payment feasibility | Can the transaction/data flow be supported in the selected geography? |

Use a simple `0 / 1 / 2` score per dimension.

Do not approve a segment only because its theoretical willingness to pay looks high.

---

# 4. Recommended first validation order

### Primary audience test

**S1 + S2** around existing digital Things / Courses.

Reason:

- lowest delivery friction;
- best fit with Thing-first model;
- allows observation of continuation / return before commerce.

### Secondary commercial test

**S3** only through one concrete Situation/Intervention hypothesis.

Do not turn the whole product into consultancy.

### Supply-side test

**S4** after Contribution / Direct Publish semantics are safe in runtime.

# 5. B2C / B2B boundary

Do not choose one universal business label yet.

Dementor may have:

```text
B2C USER = PAYER
B2B COMPANY = PAYER / PERSON OR TEAM = USER
PARTNER = PAYER / AUDIENCE = USER
```

Business priority must be decided per value object.

The product itself remains Thing-first in all cases.

Before commercial launch, choose one operating scope for each experiment:

```text
DATA PROCESSING GEO
FULFILLMENT GEO
```

These may differ.

Do not use `Russian-speaking` as a legal/payment geography.

# 7. TAM / SAM / SOM method

Do not publish a market-size number until the first market definition is approved.

After approval:

## TAM

All people / organizations matching the broad job and geography that could plausibly purchase the relevant value class.

## SAM

Subset reachable with the chosen language, channels, payment/legal setup and product Forms.

## SOM

Near-term obtainable market constrained by:

- current distribution reach;
- editorial / fulfillment capacity;
- launch geography;
- offer availability;
- realistic acquisition bandwidth.

For Dementor, capacity may make SOM much smaller than demand. That is not a problem; it is a planning truth.

---

# 8. Research plan

For each primary segment collect:

- 10–15 problem/context interviews;
- 20–50 qualified Thing experiences;
- source/intent/entry behavior;
- Thing→Thing continuation;
- return within expectation window;
- explicit value-language from users;
- paid-intent only around a concrete offer.

Avoid asking:

> Would you join this club?

Prefer:

> What was worth your attention?
> What would you want next?
> Would you choose/pay for this specific experience?

---

# 9. Approval gate

A first ICP can move from hypothesis to **WORKING MARKET SEGMENT** when:

1. the job repeats across independent people;
2. a real Thing produces qualified experience;
3. continuation / return appears without coercion;
4. the segment can be reached repeatedly;
5. a plausible value object exists for later monetization;
6. team capacity can serve it.

Until then:

> **ICP = HYPOTHESIS, NOT BRAND COPY.**
