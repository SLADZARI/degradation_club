# DEMENTOR CLUB — FINANCIAL MODEL / UNIT ECONOMICS / CAPACITY v1

Status: **DRAFT / BUSINESS PLANNING FRAMEWORK**  
Updated: **2026-09-15**

## Purpose

Define how Dementor should evaluate financial viability without inventing LTV or margin before real paid behavior exists.

---

# 1. Cost buckets

Track separately:

## Acquisition

- paid media;
- partner/referral fees;
- attributable sales cost.

## Production / editorial

- research;
- writing/design;
- development;
- media production;
- editorial review;
- creator/Dementor time.

## Fulfillment

- facilitation;
- venue;
- manufacturing;
- shipping;
- support;
- refunds/replacements.

## Payment

- processor fees;
- FX;
- platform transaction fees;
- taxes where attributable.

## Fixed platform / team

- hosting;
- software;
- core salaries/contractors;
- administration;
- legal/accounting.

Do not hide production cost inside CAC.

---

# 2. Offer-level contribution

For each paid value object calculate:

```text
NET REVENUE
- PAYMENT COST
- VARIABLE FULFILLMENT COST
- DIRECT VARIABLE PRODUCTION COST
= CONTRIBUTION BEFORE FIXED COST
```

Where production is mostly fixed/editorial, also report:

```text
CONTRIBUTION AFTER ALLOCATED PRODUCTION
```

but do not pretend allocations are cash truth.

---

# 3. Required actual inputs

Before financial approval of an offer record:

- price;
- expected capacity;
- minimum viable capacity;
- variable cost per user/unit;
- fixed delivery cost;
- production effort hours;
- payment fee;
- expected refund/failure allowance;
- tax treatment if known;
- acquisition mode.

---

# 4. Break-even

For bounded offer:

```text
BREAK-EVEN UNITS
=
FIXED OFFER COST
/
(PRICE - VARIABLE COST PER UNIT)
```

For Event also compare:

- venue capacity;
- operational capacity;
- break-even attendance.

If break-even attendance is too close to maximum capacity, offer is fragile.

---

# 5. Capacity economics

Demand is not enough.

Every offer has capacity type:

- near-zero marginal digital;
- facilitated group;
- fixed-seat Event;
- one-to-one Intervention;
- physical inventory.

Track:

```text
DEMAND
vs
FULFILLMENT CAPACITY
vs
EDITORIAL/PRODUCTION CAPACITY
```

High-demand high-touch work may be economically worse than moderate-demand scalable work.

---

# 6. Scenario model

Maintain three scenarios only when assumptions are explicit:

- conservative;
- base;
- strong.

Each must show:

- audience/paid volume assumption;
- price;
- variable cost;
- contribution;
- fixed cost;
- cash requirement;
- key capacity constraint.

Do not present scenario revenue as forecast until a base rate exists.

---

# 7. LTV discipline

Do not calculate official LTV until repeat behavior exists.

Before repeat use:

- AOV;
- paid usage;
- fulfillment;
- repeat purchase rate;
- time to repeat;
- contribution per transaction.

Once recurring/repurchase cohorts exist, use cohort-based LTV.

Simple formulas may be sensitivity tools only.

---

# 8. CAC / payback gate

Paid CAC is relevant only when:

- a real paid offer exists;
- attribution is adequate;
- fulfillment works;
- unit contribution is known.

Payback:

```text
CAC / contribution per customer per period
```

for recurring offers, only after recurring value is real.

Do not scale acquisition merely because top-line ROAS looks positive.

---

# 9. Cash / runway

Before commerce scale, maintain a 12-month cash view:

```text
OPENING CASH
+ CASH IN
- FIXED COST
- PRODUCTION INVESTMENT
- FULFILLMENT CASH OUT
- TAX / PAYMENT OUT
= CLOSING CASH
```

Physical production and Events require explicit working-capital timing.

Preorder may reduce working capital only if legally/commercially valid and fulfillment is credible.

---

# 10. Business viability gates

## One-off offer viable

- positive contribution at plausible volume;
- fulfillment quality sustainable;
- break-even below realistic capacity;
- no hidden founder labor assumption.

## Recurring offer viable

Additionally:

- repeat/retention observed;
- recurring fulfillment cost known;
- cancellation/refund behavior known;
- capacity scales with subscriber count.

## Paid acquisition viable

Additionally:

- contribution supports CAC;
- payback acceptable to cash position;
- paid cohorts preserve usage/return quality.
