# Dementor Club — Voice & Language v0.1

Status: CANDIDATE · TEST REQUIRED
Updated: 2026-09-01
Authority: editorial candidate

## 1. Purpose

This document governs how Dementor Club sounds in Russian. It does not define facts, product status or project semantics.

Default voice: calm, observant, dry, human, slightly institutional when useful, never pompous by accident.

The club sounds like a person who noticed the system is strange and decided not to shout about it.

## 2. Core language qualities

Prefer:
- short declarative sentences;
- concrete nouns and actions;
- everyday Russian;
- precise institutional vocabulary when it creates contrast;
- understatement;
- dry confidence;
- unexpected but natural naming;
- clean rhythm;
- silence after the turn.

Avoid:
- pseudo-philosophical fog;
- motivational rhetoric;
- generic brand inspiration;
- overexplaining;
- excessive adjectives;
- fake youth slang;
- translated English meme syntax;
- artificial aphorisms;
- constant cynicism;
- announcing irony.

## 3. Register system

Dementor may move between registers when the collision reveals meaning:

- bureaucratic <-> domestic;
- corporate <-> emotional;
- therapeutic <-> administrative;
- scientific <-> primitive;
- heroic <-> mundane;
- sacred <-> logistical;
- technical <-> bodily;
- premium/luxury <-> ordinary weakness.

Register collision is a tool, not a mandatory signature. Do not turn every text into a fake ministry.

## 4. Sentence behavior

Good default:
- one thought per sentence;
- important turn late but not buried;
- no filler before the observation;
- use punctuation normally;
- line breaks only when rhythm or surface requires them.

For short forms, prefer a formulation that can survive without explanation.

## 5. Lexical behavior

Prefer words people actually use in work, apps, relationships, shopping and self-development.

Specific beats abstract:
- `поставил напоминание отдохнуть` beats `общество требует эффективного отдыха`;
- `серия дней без пропусков` beats `геймификация отдыха` when the former carries the joke itself.

Anglicisms are allowed when they are real audience vocabulary or the object of satire: KPI, deadline, premium, onboarding, AI, dashboard, sprint, red flag. Do not add them for fashionable texture.

## 6. Voice position

Before writing, identify who is speaking:
- witness;
- participant;
- exhausted insider;
- administrator;
- analyst;
- seller;
- believer;
- dissident;
- Dementor Club implicating itself.

Default public voice: witness / exhausted insider.

## 7. Degree of aggression

Default aggression: LOW–MEDIUM.

Target structures and shared behavior more often than individuals. Sharpness is allowed when it points upward at institutions, industries, power, manipulative systems or the club itself.

Do not make vulnerability itself the joke.

## 8. Profanity and slang

Profanity is not part of the default brand voice. It may appear when:
- a real character/persona requires it;
- community context supports it;
- the word carries comic precision impossible to reproduce cleanly.

Never use profanity as a substitute for punch, youthfulness or authenticity.

## 9. Anti-LLM patterns

Rewrite when copy relies on:
- `В современном мире...`;
- `Мы живём в эпоху...`;
- `Это не просто X — это Y`;
- `Когда ты настолько...`;
- `Ирония в том, что...`;
- `Представьте себе...` as automatic setup;
- inflated triads and motivational cadence;
- repeated em-dash revelations;
- conclusions that restate the joke;
- interchangeable sarcasm.

Human test: `Could a specific person naturally say/write this exact sentence?`

If no, simplify or make it more specific.

## 10. Surface modulation

Home: clear first, witty second.
About: thoughtful, self-aware, low joke density.
Social: fastest, most current, most permissive.
Comments: conversational, almost no setup.
Community: allows lore and callbacks.
Projects: obey project-specific editorial voice.
Courses/games: playful but functional.
Merch: quote-level compression.
UI: character without ambiguity.
Legal/payment/support: neutral clarity.

## 11. Wrong -> Right diagnostics

Wrong: `В современном мире даже отдых становится инструментом продуктивности.`
Why: abstract, editorialized, generic.
Right direction: `Поставил напоминание не забыть отдохнуть.`

Wrong: `Это не прокрастинация — это стратегическая пауза.`
Why: reusable generic formula.
Right direction: name the exact behavior, cost or ritual rather than relabeling it automatically.

Wrong: `ИИ уже знает нас лучше, чем мы сами.`
Why: generic tech aphorism.
Right direction: identify the exact absurd interaction, permission, notification, metric or delegation.

## 12. Editorial law

Meaning before style.
Observation before cleverness.
Specificity before abstraction.
Human rhythm before brand mannerism.
Silence before explanation.

A recognisable Dementor voice is a consequence of consistent judgment, not repeated catchphrases.
