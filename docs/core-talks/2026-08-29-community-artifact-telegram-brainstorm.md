## Why this talk matters

The discussion shifts Community from a passive page/roster toward a possible **post-onboarding action loop**:

`onboarding → member → first artifact → visible community trace → reaction → discussion`

The core problem articulated in the talk is not “which Telegram chat do we need?” but:

> **What immediate micro-value / micro-action does a new club member receive and perform after onboarding, and how does that action become a persistent part of the community?**

## Hypotheses extracted from the talk

1. **Current scope is closed Community after onboarding.** An open club/community is explicitly unresolved and must not be implemented or described as existing.
2. A new member needs **immediate micro-value / micro-action** after onboarding.
3. Candidate action: create a persistent **member artifact / “Dementor notice”** on an infinite board.
4. The artifact remains attached to a specific member and becomes part of their community history.
5. Other members can discover and react to artifacts; later the platform may support filters / alternate views / feed behavior.
6. A sufficiently interesting artifact may trigger a discussion thread.
7. Telegram is being considered as a practical **discussion runtime**, not the canonical database.
8. Platform and Telegram should share naming and identity so that the same entity is recognizable in DB, web UI and chat.
9. Automatic platform → Telegram publication is a promising hypothesis.
10. Project-specific chats/topics may emerge later, but are not approved as an MVP requirement.
11. Architectural intent: **design for expansion, implement the smallest gate now.**

## Important non-decisions

The talk does **not** approve:

- an open/public Community;
- a specific Telegram channel/group/topic topology;
- project-specific chats for every project;
- artifact visibility rules;
- automatic publication triggers;
- member vs Dementor permission model;
- a course/product called “Слабоумие и отвага”;
- any public membership promise.

## Open questions for next decision session

- Is completion of onboarding sufficient to become a member, or is a separate membership confirmation required?
- What is the canonical object name: `Artifact`, `Notice`, `Proposal`, something intentionally club-specific, or a generic object with presentation variants?
- Is the first artifact mandatory, optional, or just the primary CTA after onboarding?
- Who can see an artifact by default?
- What triggers Telegram discussion: publish, first reaction, reaction threshold, or explicit “Discuss” action?
- Does Telegram contain one closed Community supergroup with Topics, or multiple groups/chats?
- Do project spaces exist immediately or only when there is real activity?
- Are Telegram reactions/comments mirrored back to the platform or treated as ephemeral discussion signals?
- How does a member contact an artifact author without exposing private contact information?
