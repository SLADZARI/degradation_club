## 0. Purpose

Humor is not decoration and not a required punchline. Dementor humor is a method for discovering and expressing contradictions in contemporary life while preserving the club's meaning, factual integrity and surface-specific clarity.

Core pipeline:

`REALITY -> SOCIAL SCRIPT -> STRANGENESS -> COMIC ANGLE -> FORM -> WORDING -> COMPRESSION -> TEST`

`PUNCHLINE` is optional. It is one ending mode among several.

## 1. Brand position

Dementor Club is inside the same absurd system as its audience. The default position is recognition, not superiority.

Primary targets: systems, norms, rituals, industries, management language, self-help language, optimization culture, consumer promises, technology rituals and our own behaviour inside them.

The first desired reaction is: `Чёрт, ведь действительно так.` The second may be laughter.

## 2. Read-before-write router

Humor Engine never works as an isolated encyclopedia. Before writing, resolve context in this order:

1. BRAND — `content/pages/about.md`.
2. SURFACE — read the relevant surface authority.
3. PROJECT / ENTITY — read its canonical source when applicable.
4. SPHERE — resolve one primary DC9 sphere.
5. VOICE — `docs/DEMENTOR_VOICE_AND_LANGUAGE_v0.1.md`.
6. HUMOR — this document.
7. FACTS — `docs/PUBLISHING_PLAYBOOK_v1.md` + `content/ENTITY_CONTRACT.md` for governed entities.
8. CORPUS — retrieve nearest approved examples when available.
9. OUTPUT — draft only after context is resolved.

Surface routing:
- Home -> `content/pages/home.md`
- About -> `content/pages/about.md`
- UI -> `docs/DIA_BEHAVIOUR_SYSTEM_v1.md`
- social visual -> `assets/social/README.md`
- merch -> canonical merch records
- project -> its own editorial source branch
- course/event/community/entity -> responsible source + Publishing Playbook + Entity Contract
- visual/editorial composition -> `docs/DEMENTOR_DESIGN_CANON_CURRENT.md`

## 3. Comic angle before mechanism

After finding a contradiction, generate at least three materially different comic angles before wording.

A comic angle answers: `From what unexpected but coherent position can this reality be interpreted?`

Examples of angle families:
- bureaucracy;
- commerce;
- status/achievement;
- technology;
- domestic reality;
- therapy/self-help;
- state/institution;
- science/measurement;
- religion/ritual;
- logistics/operations;
- child logic;
- exhausted insider;
- literal rule-follower.

Do not produce three paraphrases of the same joke and call them three options.

## 4. Ending modes

Choose an ending based on the form. Allowed modes:

- `PUNCH` — explicit semantic turn;
- `OBSERVATION` — the precise formulation is the ending;
- `NAMING` — a new label closes the piece;
- `IMPLICATION` — audience completes the conclusion;
- `ANTI_CLIMAX` — large frame ends in a trivial result;
- `VISUAL_REVEAL` — image carries the final turn;
- `CALLBACK` — prior rule/object returns with changed meaning;
- `NO_ENDING` — poster/merch/interface line is self-contained.

Do not force a punchline into a form that does not need one.

## 5. Core mechanisms

Primary mechanisms may be combined when the format supports it, but one should remain dominant.

### M01 SOCIAL CONTRADICTION
Declared rule vs actual behaviour.

### M02 REGISTER COLLISION
High/low, official/domestic, corporate/emotional, therapeutic/administrative, scientific/primitive, heroic/mundane.

### M03 INVERSION
Reverse a value hierarchy while preserving internal logic.

### M04 LITERALIZATION
Apply metaphor, slogan or professional phrase literally.

### M05 NAMING
Invent a compact reusable label for a familiar behaviour or phenomenon.

### M06 LOGICAL OVERCOMMITMENT
Take a reasonable rule and apply it too consistently until its absurd implication appears.

### M07 BUREAUCRATIZATION
Turn a human act into a procedure, registration, regulation or compliance workflow.

### M08 COMMERCIALIZATION
Turn a human state, relationship or weakness into product/tier/upsell.

### M09 TECHNOLOGIZATION
Give AI, app, dashboard or automation to a problem that did not need one.

### M10 STATUS INVERSION
Describe refusal, failure, weakness or reduced ambition with achievement language.

### M11 HYPERBOLE / ESCALATION
Push a real behaviour beyond its reasonable boundary.

### M12 UNDERSTATEMENT
Describe significant absurdity as a minor operational detail.

### M13 PSEUDO-DOCUMENT
Memo, policy, warning, protocol, certificate, vacancy, report, technical spec.

### M14 DIALOGIC REFRAME
Second voice interprets first in an unexpected but internally valid way.

### M15 CALLBACK / RUNNING GAG
Return known phrase/object/rule with compounded meaning.

### M16 WORLD / LORE LOGIC
Build stable absurd institutions, characters and rules with newcomer entry points.

### M17 CULTURAL ALLUSION
Use a shared cultural script only when it adds meaning and decode cost is appropriate.

### M18 SELF-IMPLICATION
Club/author/persona is also trapped by the same contradiction.

## 6. Voice position

VOICE is not enough. Resolve `VOICE_POSITION`:

- believer — sincerely accepts the absurd rule;
- administrator — enforces it;
- seller — monetizes it;
- analyst — measures it;
- participant — suffers inside it;
- exhausted insider — complies without enthusiasm;
- witness — calmly observes;
- dissident — breaks rule without sermonizing;
- club self-implication — admits Dementor does the same thing.

Default: calm witness or exhausted insider.

## 7. Cultural pack

Before using allusion, slang, platform language or generational code, identify required shared knowledge.

`CONTEXT COST`:
- LOW — understandable without insider knowledge; default for Home/About/public onboarding.
- MEDIUM — common professional/platform/cultural code; acceptable for social/projects.
- HIGH — internal lore, niche meme or recurring character; acceptable mainly for Community and series.

Do not require HIGH context on entry surfaces.

## 8. Surface rules

### Home
Humor density: MEDIUM. Recognition before lore. One strong line per block. CTA clarity wins.

### About
LOW–MEDIUM. Philosophy must survive if jokes are removed. Self-implication is preferred over superiority.

### Social / memes
HIGH. Start from a fresh observation, not a template. Trend content never rewrites evergreen canon.

### Community
MEDIUM–HIGH. Callbacks, naming, characters and shared language are encouraged.

### Projects
Defined by the project's own editorial source. Humor Engine supplies mechanics, not project truth.

### Courses / games
Humor should happen through mechanics, transitions, feedback and anti-achievements without blocking comprehension.

### Merch
Maximum compression. One idea. No explanatory dependency.

### Events
Humor may shape name/program/ritual; date, place, price and instructions remain literal.

### UI
Humor may reframe wording but may not mutate canonical facts or make actions ambiguous.

### Payment / legal / support
Clarity dominates. Humor minimal or absent.

## 9. DC9 routing

Every material should have one primary sphere: Личность, Работа, Потребление, Отношения, Контроль, Информация, Саморазвитие, Смысл, Технологии.

Sphere selects the social contradiction; it does not prescribe a fixed joke form.

## 10. Generation protocol

Internally resolve:

`SURFACE`
`PRIMARY_SPHERE`
`AUDIENCE / CONTEXT_COST`
`REALITY`
`SOCIAL_SCRIPT`
`STRANGENESS`
`3 COMIC_ANGLES`
`DOMINANT_MECHANISM`
`VOICE_POSITION`
`FORM`
`ENDING_MODE`
`FACT_BOUNDARY`
`COMPRESSION`

Then write.

For a single requested output, generate multiple candidates internally and return the strongest unless the user asks for options.

## 11. Hard gates

### G1 Recognition
Is the observation true/recognizable?

### G2 Dementor DNA
Does it expose a club-relevant social construction rather than generic sarcasm?

### G3 Generic transplant test
If the topic nouns are replaced and the same joke still works in any meme page, reject or rewrite.

### G4 Specificity
Is there a concrete behaviour, object, phrase, interface, ritual or consequence?

### G5 Surprise
Is the interpretation non-obvious?

### G6 Language
Does it sound like a human Russian speaker rather than a generated aphorism?

### G7 Compression
Can anything be removed without losing the turn?

### G8 Fact integrity
Humor may reframe facts. Humor may not mutate facts.

### G9 Explanation
If the joke needs explanation, it is not ready.

### G10 Surface fitness
Would the same wording still be right if moved to another surface? If yes, surface adaptation is probably insufficient.

`GENERIC = REJECT` is a hard rule.

## 12. Anti-AI filter

Warning patterns:
- mechanical `Когда ты настолько X, что Y`;
- repeated `Это не X, это Y`;
- generic `Я / также я`;
- motivational cliché with one antonym replaced;
- universal sarcastic aphorism;
- three options that are only paraphrases;
- fake profundity;
- excessive explanatory setup;
- English meme cadence translated word-for-word.

## 13. Corpus learning

Approved/rejected real materials must be recorded in `content/editorial/humor-corpus.json` using `content/editorial/humor-corpus.schema.json`.

Corpus is evidence of editorial taste, not automatic authority. Human approval remains decisive.

Prefer retrieval by:
`surface + sphere + comic_angle + mechanism + voice_position`.

Never blindly imitate wording from the corpus. Learn transformations, density and rejection reasons.
