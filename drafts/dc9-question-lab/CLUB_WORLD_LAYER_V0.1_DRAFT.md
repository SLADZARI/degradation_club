# DC-9 — Club World Layer v0.1

Status: **PRODUCT / EDITORIAL HYPOTHESIS / NOT CANON / NOT PRODUCTION**  
Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Some DC-9 questions may take place inside the real Dementor Club world so that the user gradually understands the club through experience rather than through an explanatory onboarding block.

This is **not advertising inside the quiz**.

The primary job of every scored screen remains diagnostic evidence for its existing tag or guard.

Club World is a secondary contextual layer only.

## Core rule

> **Сначала хороший диагностический эпизод. Потом — знакомство с клубом.**

A Club World scene must still work for a person who has never heard of the referenced object/project/event.

If understanding the question requires prior club knowledge, the placement fails.

## Recommended density

Do not distribute placement symmetrically across all nine spheres.

Working target:

- approximately `4–7` scored Club World scenes across all `54` screens;
- irregular distribution;
- never two obvious placements in a row;
- no requirement that every sphere contain one.

This is intentionally below a visible marketing cadence.

## Allowed source material

Only real, approved Club entities may be used publicly:

- approved Merch / artifacts;
- Community as an approved ecosystem pillar, without inventing membership mechanics;
- Offline Events as an approved ecosystem pillar, without inventing a specific event/date/price/venue;
- approved Projects;
- `Логика и осознанность` only using material verified against its independent `logic-awareness` source-of-truth;
- already approved public club language / rules / artifacts.

Do not pull brainstorm mechanics into a scored question as if they already exist.

## Forbidden

A scored Club World scene must not:

- advertise a product with a CTA;
- ask `что вам больше нравится в клубе`;
- reward familiarity with Dementor Club;
- reward agreement with club ideology;
- reveal the Club-preferred answer;
- introduce a membership rule, price, event, role or community mechanic that is not approved;
- alter the hidden tag or guard meaning in order to showcase an artifact;
- turn the quiz into a site tour.

## Placement pattern

Best pattern:

`real Club object/context → normal human contradiction → choice → dry system fixation`

Bad pattern:

`here is our product/project → explanation of what it means → do you agree?`

## v0.6 initial placements

### 03-Q2 · Consumption / #непокупка

Real artifact:

`OBJECT 001 — НЕ НАДО`

Why it fits:

The product itself creates the contradiction: the user is looking at a product card whose message is literally `НЕ НАДО`, while the purchasing impulse continues.

The object is scene material, not a CTA.

### 08-Q1 · Meaning / #локальныйсмысл

Real ecosystem context:

Why it fits:

The scene can communicate a club value — human time does not need a productivity justification — without defining any event mechanic or promising a particular format.

## QA gate for a Club World question

Before approval:

1. Does the question still measure the original tag/guard?
2. Would the question work for a stranger to the Club?
3. Is the Club entity real and approved?
4. Is there zero need to explain the entity?
5. Does the scene reveal culture through behavior rather than description?
6. Is there no CTA or sales language?
7. Is no answer more desirable merely because it sounds more loyal to the Club?
8. Does removing the brand/entity leave the diagnostic conflict intact?

If `8 = no`, the placement is probably carrying too much diagnostic meaning and should be reconsidered.

## Product principle

The desired user effect is not:

> `Теперь я знаю, какие разделы есть у Dementor Club.`

It is:

> `Я уже несколько раз оказался внутри этого странного мира и начинаю понимать, как он устроен.`

The quiz should therefore work as:

`diagnostic experience → comedy → cultural onboarding`

not:

`diagnostic → advertisement → diagnostic → advertisement`.
