## Why v0.4 exists

v0.3 solved the length / psychometric-prose problem but remained closer to an intelligent onboarding with a smile than to a strong comic scene.

The missing comedy engine was **human contradiction**:

> wanted others to cope without you → they cope without you → the successful outcome itself activates suspicion / checking.

v0.4 makes that contradiction explicit while keeping the hidden semantic construct unchanged.

## Public scene v0.4

Вы обычно собираете друзей.

На этот раз они всё сделали сами.

> Место выбрано. Стол забронирован. Даже время всех устраивает.

В чат пишут:

> **«Всё готово. Просто приходи».**

**Люди справились без вас. Пока наблюдаем.**

## Hidden construct

`role_maintenance / identity_service`

The question is not about competence, kindness or whether checking reservations is objectively sensible.

It tests whether an old identity-role keeps functioning when the external need for it disappears.

## Human Contradiction Gate

### What did the person want?

A normal organizer repeatedly wants other people to be able to handle things without requiring them.

### What happened?

They handled everything without the person.

### What becomes uncomfortable?

The successful outcome itself becomes evidence that something may need checking.

### Comedy engine

`desired autonomy → autonomy achieved → control system reactivates`

**PASS.**

## Exact everyday detail

- place selected;
- table booked;
- time works for everyone;
- short group-chat message.

The details demonstrate that no practical rescue is currently necessary.

## Dementor shift

Primary:

> **Люди справились без вас. Пока наблюдаем.**

Supporting line:

> **Даже время всех устраивает.**

The joke is no longer simply “this is suspicious”; it identifies why the normal success becomes funny.

## Quotable Line Gate

Strong candidates:

1. `Люди справились без вас. Пока наблюдаем.`
2. `Полностью исправная организация без моего участия пока не проверена практикой.`

The second is the stronger standalone line; the first is the better scene-level line.

**PASS.**

## 0 / 1 boundary

## 2 / 3 boundary

- `2` — role returns as a repeatable verification mechanism;
- `3` — continued involvement is justified as a general principle / model of how reliable organization works.
