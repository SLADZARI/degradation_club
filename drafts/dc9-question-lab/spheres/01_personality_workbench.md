This workbench deliberately separates **editorial pattern analysis** from **canonical scoring**.

## Gold-standard coverage target

The four thematic scenes must measure different forms of self-relation:

1. role maintenance;
2. value through activity / unused free time;
3. self-image defense;
4. change without identity rebranding.

Two behavioral guards then test:

5. intentionality under a profitable/easy exception;
6. responsibility after a prior commitment/consequence already exists.

Optional scenes are added only if one of these six dimensions lacks sufficient evidence.

# Q1 — ROLE MAINTENANCE

## Scene seed

You have spent years being the person who “always organizes everything”.

This time nobody asked you to organize anything.

The event is approaching normally without you.

## What exactly are we measuring?

Whether a person can stop servicing an identity after the environment no longer requires the role.

Primary mechanism candidate: `identity_service / role_maintenance`.

## What separates 0 from 1?

To define during answer writing.

Target distinction:

- direct acceptance that the role is not needed;
- ordinary social adaptation where the person still checks, explains or lightly maintains involvement.

## What separates 2 from 3?

To define during answer writing.

Target distinction:

- repeatable mechanism for preserving the role;
- principled belief that “if I can organize it, I should” / identity-level justification.

## Price of choice

Current scene is low-to-medium stake unless we add a concrete social/status consequence.

Candidate impact: `I2–I3`.

## Can the Club-preferred answer be guessed?

Risk: yes, if one answer simply says “I do nothing”.

Requirement: all four answers must have credible reasons to either intervene or not intervene.

## Do answers sound like real people?

Not written yet — mandatory check before candidate status.

## Satirical payoff candidate

Technical diagnosis direction:

- `САМОПРОИЗВОЛЬНОЕ ОБСЛУЖИВАНИЕ СОБСТВЕННОЙ ФУНКЦИИ`
- `РОЛЬ ПРОДОЛЖАЕТ РАБОТАТЬ БЕЗ ВНЕШНЕГО ЗАПРОСА`

Not production copy.

## Does this add new information?

Yes if it isolates self-maintained identity, not generic boundaries or responsibility.

## Presentation candidate

`P04 Witness` or plain scene + `P09 Dry classification`.

Diagnostic function of witness: show that others are fine without the old role, removing the excuse that intervention is necessary.

# Q2 — VALUE THROUGH ACTIVITY / FREE TIME

## Scene seed

Saturday is completely free.

No overdue tasks, no promises, no one needs your help.

By noon you have done nothing useful.

## What exactly are we measuring?

Whether personal value needs to be confirmed through activity when no external obligation exists.

Primary mechanism candidate: `activity_as_self-worth / productive_self-validation`.

## What separates 0 from 1?

Target distinction:

- direct use of free time without self-justification;
- ordinary adjustment where the person adds a small useful act to make the day feel legitimate.

## What separates 2 from 3?

Target distinction:

- structured system for turning free time back into productive time;
- principle-level belief that unproductive time is waste / personal decline / failure of discipline.

## Price of choice

Low external cost, but high construct purity because reputation can be removed entirely.

Candidate impact: `I2–I3`.

## Can the Club-preferred answer be guessed?

High risk if the “lie on the sofa” answer is obviously the Club joke.

Requirement: answers must revolve around believable forms of planning, rest, light productivity and justification.

## Satirical payoff candidate

- `НЕСАНКЦИОНИРОВАННАЯ НЕЗАНЯТОСТЬ`
- `ПОЛЕЗНОСТЬ ДНЯ НЕ ПОДТВЕРЖДЕНА`

## Does this add new information?

Yes if it measures self-worth through activity rather than work habits. It must remain personality, not duplicate the Work sphere.

## Presentation candidate

`P11 Nobody will know` + `P06 No-excuse constraint`.

Diagnostic function: remove public reputation and practical obligation, leaving internal pressure to be useful.

# Q3 — SELF-IMAGE DEFENSE

## Scene seed

A close friend describes you to a new person in a way that is not insulting, but does not match how you usually think of yourself.

Example direction: you think of yourself as spontaneous; they describe you as extremely cautious.

## What exactly are we measuring?

What happens when another person’s believable description conflicts with the preferred internal image.

Primary mechanism candidate: `self_image_defense / narrative_correction`.

## What separates 0 from 1?

Target distinction:

- direct curiosity / acceptance of discrepant information;
- ordinary clarification or contextual explanation.

## What separates 2 from 3?

Target distinction:

- systematic correction of the external narrative;
- worldview-level need to preserve a coherent self-definition.

## Price of choice

Social identity / relationship perception.

Candidate impact: `I3–I4`.

## Can the Club-preferred answer be guessed?

Risk: “I accept any opinion about myself” can look therapeutically correct.

Requirement: avoid therapy language; each response should protect something real — accuracy, dignity, relationship, coherence or curiosity.

## Satirical payoff candidate

- `РАСХОЖДЕНИЕ С АРХИВНОЙ ВЕРСИЕЙ ЛИЧНОСТИ`
- `ПРОВЕДЕНА НЕЗАПЛАНИРОВАННАЯ КОРРЕКТИРОВКА САМООПИСАНИЯ`

## Does this add new information?

Yes. This is image defense under external observation, distinct from role maintenance.

## Presentation candidate

`P04 Witness`.

Diagnostic function: attack self-description through a credible external view rather than asking “how do you see yourself?”.

# Q4 — CHANGE WITHOUT REBRANDING

## Scene seed

Something you genuinely liked and defended five years ago no longer interests you.

People around you remember how strongly you identified with it.

## What exactly are we measuring?

Whether preferences can change without turning the change into a new explanatory identity or preserving consistency with the old one.

Primary mechanism candidate: `identity_consistency / rebranding_change`.

## What separates 0 from 1?

Target distinction:

- direct change in preference;
- ordinary explanation to preserve social continuity.

## What separates 2 from 3?

Target distinction:

- systematic reframing of the past so the identity remains coherent;
- principle-level belief that personal change must form a consistent narrative/concept.

## Price of choice

Identity coherence + social consistency.

Candidate impact: `I3–I4`.

## Can the Club-preferred answer be guessed?

Risk: “people change” can become the obviously wise answer.

Requirement: all four answers must plausibly protect consistency, accuracy, social intelligibility or freedom to change.

## Satirical payoff candidate

- `ОБНОВЛЕНИЕ ЛИЧНОСТИ БЕЗ CHANGELOG`
- `ПРИЧИНА ИЗМЕНЕНИЯ ВКУСА ДОКУМЕНТАЛЬНО НЕ ПОДТВЕРЖДЕНА`

## Does this add new information?

Yes if it focuses on narrative consistency, not generic imperfection.

## Presentation candidate

`P04 Witness` + optional `P05 Evidence` (old post / old statement summarized as one factual line).

Diagnostic function: concrete evidence prevents the user from pretending the old identity was never real.

# G1 — INTENTIONALITY GUARD

## Scene seed

You stopped playing an old high-status role.

Now you receive a concrete opportunity where returning to that role would bring meaningful money, status or approval.

You are fully capable of doing it.

## What exactly are we measuring?

Whether the refusal is a choice or merely exhaustion, inability, exclusion or lack of opportunity.

## Price of choice

Material benefit + status + identity.

Candidate impact: `I5–I6`.

## Anti-gaming requirement

There must be no morally superior “stay true to yourself” answer.

Returning temporarily, refusing, renegotiating and exploiting the role strategically must all be psychologically credible responses.

## Satirical payoff candidate

- `СТАРАЯ ВЕРСИЯ ДОСТУПНА ДЛЯ ВРЕМЕННОГО ВОССТАНОВЛЕНИЯ`
- `ПРОВЕДЕНА ПРОВЕРКА ОТКАЗА НА ДЕНЕЖНОЕ ВОЗНАГРАЖДЕНИЕ`

## Presentation

`P08 Profitable exception` + `P03 Stakes strip`.

# G2 — RESPONSIBILITY GUARD

## Scene seed

You have decided to stop being “the person who organizes everything”.

Before making that decision, you already promised to handle one concrete part of a shared plan.

The deadline is now close.

## What exactly are we measuring?

Whether abandoning an identity/rule is used to retroactively cancel obligations already accepted under the old role.

## Price of choice

Other people’s plans + existing commitment.

Candidate impact: `I4–I5`.

## Anti-gaming requirement

Do not ask “who is responsible?”. Put the commitment in motion and ask what happens next.

Options should include realistic forms of completion, transfer, renegotiation or unilateral abandonment without labeling any as virtuous.

## Satirical payoff candidate

- `НОВАЯ ЛИЧНОСТЬ ОБНАРУЖИЛА СТАРОЕ ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬСТВО`
- `СМЕНА РОЛИ НЕ АННУЛИРОВАЛА БРОНЬ`

## Presentation

`P07 Already happened` + optional `P12 Forced allocation`.

# Candidate sphere rhythm

1. Q1 Role maintenance — low/medium social stake.
2. Q2 Free time — reputation removed.
3. Short system transition.
4. Q3 Self-image defense — external witness.
5. Q4 Change without rebranding — archived evidence.
6. G1 Intentionality — profitable high-stake exception.
7. Short system transition.
8. G2 Responsibility — consequence/prior commitment.

This is six scored scenes plus two tiny transitions, not eight full questions.

## Duration target hypothesis

A six-scene sphere should feel like a short episode, not a questionnaire.

Editorial target before UX playtest:

- scene comprehension: ~5–10 seconds;
- answer scan + choice: ~5–12 seconds;
- total typical sphere: roughly 1.5–2.5 minutes;
- no mandatory explanatory text between every screen.

These are UX hypotheses, not approved product metrics.
