# DC-9 Question Lab — Methodology v0.4

Status: **DRAFT AUTHORING METHOD / MUST OBEY CANONICAL PRESENTATION STANDARD**  
Date: 2026-08-31

Canonical presentation authority:

- `operations/QUESTION_PRESENTATION_HUMOR_STANDARD.md` in `dementor-club`.

This document translates that canon into the working authoring process for the question lab.

## What changed from v0.3

The v0.3 hypothesis said that a premise could be fully serious and that satire could arrive mainly after the choice.

That produced questions that were diagnostically competent but too long, too explanatory and too close to psychometrics.

v0.4 changes the authoring priority:

> **The user must feel Dementor Club on the question screen, not only in the result.**

The humor may be quiet. It must not become a gag. But every normal question should contain a small Dementor shift: exact absurd detail, dry administrative observation, social contradiction or internally flawless over-logic.

## Public screen ≠ editorial workbench

The workbench may contain:

- construct;
- pattern target;
- impact;
- evidence mapping;
- rationale;
- alternative wording;
- QA notes.

The public screen must contain only:

1. what happened;
2. one detail that makes it specific;
3. the slight reality shift;
4. four ways out.

Everything else is editorial scaffolding and must stay hidden.

## Compression rule

Before writing answers, compress the scene.

Ask:

> Can a user understand the choice in 5–8 seconds?

Working target:

- premise + detail: usually 35–45 words maximum;
- answer: usually 8–16 words;
- one screen must visually feel light.

If the scene needs a paragraph of biography, replace exposition with evidence inside the current moment.

### Replace biography with behavior

Avoid:

> Вы много лет были человеком, который всегда всё организует...

Prefer:

> Вы обычно собираете друзей. На этот раз они всё сделали сами.

The construct remains hidden but observable.

## First-question rule

Q1 of every sphere is special.

It should teach the language of the quiz before it tries to maximize diagnostic depth.

Required Q1 properties:

- immediate everyday recognition;
- low or medium stake;
- very short setup;
- one concrete detail;
- one quiet Dementor shift;
- four compact answers;
- no theory;
- no biography dump;
- no explanation of why this matters.

Q1 should make the user think:

> «Окей. Понял, что за мир. Давайте следующий».

Not:

> «Сейчас меня будут анализировать».

## Scene authoring sequence

### 1. State the construct internally

Example: `role_maintenance`.

Never start public copy from the construct name.

### 2. Find a current moment

Not the person's history. A moment happening now.

Example:

> Friends already organized the evening.

### 3. Add one concrete proof

Message, time, price, booking, number, object, witness.

Example:

> «Всё готово. Просто приходи».

### 4. Add one Dementor shift

It should be dry and short.

Example:

> Это выглядит подозрительно.

### 5. Write 8–12 realistic reactions

Do not begin from score positions.

### 6. Select four answers

All four must be defensible by a real person.

Preferred public composition:

- two ordinary;
- one rational but slightly strange;
- one internally flawless over-extension.

This composition is not tied to hidden score order.

### 7. Attach hidden semantics only after wording works

Then assign:

- `pattern_id`;
- `pattern_stage`;
- `canonical_evidence` candidate;
- impact;
- rationale.

Do not let hidden semantics make one answer longer, wiser or more articulate.

## Pattern-stage hypothesis remains separate

Internal editorial stages remain experimental:

- 0 — direct response;
- 1 — ordinary adaptation;
- 2 — repeatable mechanism;
- 3 — mechanism turned into principle/worldview.

`pattern_stage` is descriptive, not moral and not automatically the Dementor score.

`canonical_evidence` remains a separate experimental mapping layer.

## Humor placement

Use three levels:

### Scene humor

Mandatory in most thematic questions, even if minimal.

Examples:

- `Даже время всех устраивает.`
- `Исправление биографии пока никто не запросил.`
- `Ботинки исправны. Горы претензий не предъявляли.`

### Answer humor

Optional and dangerous.

An answer may be funny because its logic goes too far, but it must remain a real choice.

### System humor

Short administrative fixation after selected questions, not mechanically after all six.

The strongest joke can still live in the result, but the journey must not feel like a serious assessment until then.

## Question rhythm inside one sphere

Recommended:

1. quick / funny / low-stake entry;
2. recognizable social scene;
3. stronger identity or factual conflict;
4. slightly stranger scene;
5. profitable or already-happened stress test;
6. responsibility guard, mostly serious.

Do not optimize every scene for maximum absurdity.

## Impact I1–I7

Impact remains based on:

1. cost of being wrong;
2. personal stake.

Working coefficients remain experimental:

`I1=.70 · I2=.80 · I3=.90 · I4=1.00 · I5=1.15 · I6=1.30 · I7=1.50`

Do not inflate scene length to justify impact. Impact lives in the consequence, not in explanatory prose.

## Strong anti-self-report patterns

Keep:

- `already happened`;
- `nobody will know`;
- `profitable exception`;
- witness;
- hard fact / receipt;
- forced allocation.

But present them compactly.

## Mandatory per-question authoring table

| Check | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| What exactly are we measuring? | One hidden mechanism |
| What is happening right now? | One current scene, not biography |
| What one detail proves it? | Message/time/price/object/fact |
| Where is the Dementor shift? | One short line/detail |
| Can premise be cut further? | If yes, cut it |
| What separates 0/1? | Direct vs adaptation |
| What separates 2/3? | Mechanism vs principle |
| Can Club answer be guessed? | If yes, rewrite |
| Are all four real choices? | Required |
| Does humor work before result? | Required for thematic scenes |
| What later payoff exists? | System/result line |
| Does this add new evidence? | If no, delete |

## Fatal authoring failures

Reject / rewrite if:

- the public scene starts with biography or explanation;
- Q1 takes more than a few seconds to understand;
- the question could lose half its words with no loss;
- all humor is postponed to the result;
- the premise reads like psychometrics;
- hidden construct terminology leaks into public copy;
- one answer is the joke;
- one answer is visibly the Club answer;
- system reaction interprets psychology;
- diagnostic sophistication is achieved by text volume rather than scene design.

## Gold-standard goal for Personality

The sphere still covers:

1. role maintenance;
2. value through activity;
3. self-image defense;
4. change without rebranding;
5. intentionality guard;
6. responsibility guard.

But the public experience should feel like **six quick incidents**, not six case studies.

## Authoring mantra

> **Не объяснять конструкцию. Показать момент.**  
> **Не добавлять юмор после теста. Слегка испортить реальность уже сейчас.**  
> **Не доказывать глубину текстом. Доказывать её выбором.**
