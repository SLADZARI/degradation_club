# DC-9 Pattern Taxonomy v0.1

Status: **DRAFT EDITORIAL TAXONOMY / NOT CANON / NOT PSYCHOLOGICAL TYPOLOGY**  
Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

The question lab needs a shared language for describing **how a person handles friction in a scene** without turning the quiz into a moral ladder or a personality test.

The working question is:

> What mechanism is the person using here to deal with reality, discomfort, uncertainty, status, responsibility, loss or change?

These labels are internal editorial tools only.

They are NOT:

- personality types;
- diagnoses;
- permanent traits;
- a tenth DC-9 axis;
- public result labels by default.

A user may express different mechanisms in different spheres and even inside one sphere.

## Two-layer description

Every answer may carry two independent descriptors:

1. `pattern_id` — **what kind of mechanism is present**;
2. `pattern_stage 0–3` — **how fully that mechanism has become a repeatable system/principle**.

Neither field is automatically the canonical sphere score.

## Pattern stage

- `0` — direct reaction; no protective construction is required;
- `1` — ordinary explanation / social adaptation;
- `2` — repeatable protective mechanism;
- `3` — mechanism is defended as a principle, identity or worldview.

## Initial cross-sphere mechanism families

These are candidate families for editorial reuse. They may be split, renamed or rejected after question-bank work.

### PT01 — DIRECT_ACTION

The person acts on the current facts without first constructing an explanation, defense or identity around the action.

Examples:
- changes a decision when circumstances changed;
- says no without manufacturing a superior philosophy;
- stops using something that no longer works.

Diagnostic risk: directness is not automatically healthy or Dementor-aligned. Context decides canonical meaning.

### PT02 — SOCIAL_EXPLANATION

The person can act, but first softens, explains or legitimizes the action for themselves or others.

Examples:
- gives a respectable reason for declining;
- explains why an old opinion changed;
- keeps a role lightly available through politeness.

Typical stage: `1`, but not always.

### PT03 — IMAGE_PROTECTION

Behavior is shaped by the need to preserve a preferred description of oneself.

Examples:
- repairs reputation after an awkward moment;
- hides evidence that contradicts the self-image;
- avoids a preference because it does not fit the established identity.

Useful spheres: Personality, Relationships, Consumption, Work.

### PT04 — ROLE_MAINTENANCE

A familiar function continues to operate even after the environment no longer requires it.

Examples:
- continues organizing when others already organized;
- remains the unofficial rescuer after delegation;
- keeps monitoring because “someone has to”.

Useful spheres: Personality, Work, Control.

### PT05 — CONTROL_BUFFER

The person creates checks, backups, monitoring or reserve plans to reduce uncertainty.

Examples:
- silently rechecks completed work;
- keeps a fallback option “just in case”;
- delays release until enough assurance accumulates.

Important: this can be competent risk management. It becomes diagnostically interesting only when the scene clarifies cost and necessity.

### PT06 — RESPONSIBILITY_REDISTRIBUTION

The person changes who owns the consequence, obligation or failure.

Examples:
- asks which current priority should be removed for an urgent task;
- makes an owner explicit before proceeding;
- transfers a previously accepted commitment after changing role.

This is not automatically avoidance. It may be responsible architecture or responsibility evasion depending on the exact behavior.

### PT07 — UNCERTAINTY_DELAY

Action is postponed while waiting for more certainty, more inputs or a cleaner decision environment.

Examples:
- “сохраняю пространство для дополнительных вводных”;
- keeps research open after enough evidence exists;
- avoids closure by redefining delay as prudence.

Useful spheres: Control, Information, Meaning.

### PT08 — SUNK_COST_DEFENSE

Past investment becomes a reason to continue even after current value has disappeared.

Examples:
- finishes a useless paid course because money was spent;
- keeps an unused expensive object;
- continues a project to justify prior effort.

Useful spheres: Consumption, Self-development, Work, Meaning.

### PT09 — STATUS_SERVICE

Choice is shaped by how success, competence, taste or seriousness will be read by others.

Examples:
- upgrades an object to remain legible in the environment;
- takes a role because refusing looks like decline;
- maintains an opinion because changing it publicly costs status.

Useful spheres: Consumption, Personality, Work, Information.

### PT10 — OPTIMIZATION_LOOP

A workable state is treated as insufficient because improvement itself remains obligatory.

Examples:
- measures something that does not require action;
- upgrades a system without a concrete problem;
- turns every imperfection into a project.

Useful spheres: Self-development, Technology, Work, Personality.

### PT11 — PRINCIPLE_JUSTIFICATION

A local coping mechanism has acquired a general theory explaining why this is the correct way to live or work.

This family often appears at `pattern_stage=3` but should usually be paired with the concrete mechanism it justifies.

Examples:
- `ROLE_MAINTENANCE + PRINCIPLE_JUSTIFICATION`;
- `UNCERTAINTY_DELAY + PRINCIPLE_JUSTIFICATION`;
- `OPTIMIZATION_LOOP + PRINCIPLE_JUSTIFICATION`.

### PT12 — EXIT_WITHOUT_REPAIR

The person stops maintaining a role, process, purchase, position or explanation after it no longer serves the present situation.

This is useful as an editorial description but MUST NOT be treated as globally “good”.

A clean exit may be sphere-aligned in one scene and irresponsible in another, especially when prior commitments remain.

## Compound patterns

Answers may use more than one pattern when necessary, but do not create a bag of labels.

Preferred:

`primary_pattern: ROLE_MAINTENANCE`
`secondary_pattern: CONTROL_BUFFER`

Avoid assigning four or five mechanisms to one answer. If that seems necessary, the scene is probably too broad.

## Relation to canonical evidence

Pattern family and stage are descriptive.

Canonical evidence remains sphere-specific.

Example in Personality / role maintenance:

- `EXIT_WITHOUT_REPAIR` after no obligation exists may align strongly with refusal to service identity;
- the same behavior after a prior promise may fail the responsibility guard.

Therefore:

> **Pattern ≠ score. Context + sphere construct determine diagnostic evidence.**

## Satirical use

The public result may later transform internal mechanisms into technical administrative language, for example:

- `ROLE_MAINTENANCE` → `РОЛЬ ПРОДОЛЖАЕТ ФУНКЦИОНИРОВАТЬ БЕЗ ВНЕШНЕГО ЗАПРОСА`;
- `CONTROL_BUFFER` → `ОБНАРУЖЕН РЕЗЕРВНЫЙ КОНТУР ПРОВЕРКИ`;
- `UNCERTAINTY_DELAY` → `РЕШЕНИЕ ОСТАВЛЕНО ОТКРЫТЫМ ДО ПОЯВЛЕНИЯ ДОПОЛНИТЕЛЬНОЙ РЕАЛЬНОСТИ`;
- `SUNK_COST_DEFENSE` → `ПРЕДЫДУЩИЕ ЗАТРАТЫ ДОПУЩЕНЫ К УПРАВЛЕНИЮ БУДУЩИМ`.

These are tone examples, not approved public result axes.

## Editorial rule

A good question should make the user choose between plausible mechanisms, not between virtue and stupidity.

A good result should let the user recognize the mechanism **after** the choice and laugh at its bureaucratic classification.
