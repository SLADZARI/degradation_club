# DC-9 Question Lab — Methodology v0.3

Status: **DRAFT HYPOTHESIS / NOT PRODUCTION CANON**  
Date: 2026-08-31

## Why this document exists

The production DC-9 canon currently treats thematic answer scores as a monotonic measure of how strongly a choice stops serving a successful-success construction. That contract remains untouched in `dementor-club` until explicitly approved.

This draft explores a different editorial model for question writing:

> We do not ask how "good a Dementor" a person is. We observe how a person protects themselves from reality, discomfort, loss of status, ambiguity, responsibility or change.

The purpose of the hypothesis is to reduce the visible moral ladder `0 = bad / 3 = correct Dementor answer` and make every answer psychologically plausible.

## The three-layer question rule

Every candidate question should contain three layers.

### 1. Ordinary life scene

A recognizable situation written almost seriously.

No joke is required in the premise. The user should recognize the situation before recognizing Dementor Club.

### 2. Four plausible reactions

All four answers must sound like things an intelligent real person might actually do.

No answer should exist only to be obviously foolish, irresponsible or ideologically wrong.

### 3. Hidden systemic absurdity

The satire is primarily delivered after or around the choice: through a transition line, administrative classification, technical diagnosis or final result.

A normal human strategy can later receive an absurdly formal Dementor diagnosis.

Example principle:

> A good Dementor Club question does not make the user choose a funny answer. It makes the user choose a normal answer that later becomes a funny diagnosis.

## Pattern stage hypothesis

For editorial work, each answer can be tagged with a hidden `pattern_stage`:

- `0` — direct reaction: the person acts, acknowledges, changes or answers without building a protective construction around the action;
- `1` — ordinary adaptation: the person explains, softens or socially adjusts the action, but no stable mechanism is present;
- `2` — structured protective pattern: the person repeatedly uses a mechanism to manage discomfort, risk, image, uncertainty or responsibility;
- `3` — worldview-level pattern: the mechanism has become a principle the person can defend as the correct way to live.

Important: **this is not yet an approved replacement for the canonical answer score.**

A `pattern_stage=3` is not automatically a high Dementor sphere score. It may represent a strong sphere-relevant refusal, or it may represent an entrenched avoidance mechanism that should reduce or redirect the final interpretation. The mapping must be validated per sphere before any production scoring change.

## Why this distinction matters

The current test can become gameable if the most sophisticated, longest or most philosophically Dementor-sounding answer is always the highest score.

The pattern model changes the editorial question from:

> Which answer is the most Dementor?

into:

> What strategy is the person using here, and how fully has it become a system?

This lets all options remain credible and creates satire from classification rather than caricature.

## Gold-standard sphere structure

For `01 · Personality`, use four thematic scenes that measure genuinely different forms of self-relation:

1. **Role maintenance** — can the person stop servicing an identity they built?
2. **Value through activity** — does free time need to prove personal worth?
3. **Self-image defense** — what happens when another person describes them differently from their preferred self-image?
4. **Change without rebranding** — can preferences change without requiring a new explanatory identity?

Then use two behavioral guards:

5. **Intentionality** — does the pattern survive when returning to the old behavior is profitable/easy?
6. **Responsibility** — does abandoning a role or rule cancel previously accepted consequences and commitments?

Optional questions are allowed only when they add independent evidence.

## The 2 vs 3 editorial boundary

This boundary must be explicit in every candidate question.

- `2` = the person has a repeatable mechanism;
- `3` = the mechanism has become a principle, identity or explanatory worldview.

The answer at 3 should not merely be better written, calmer, more mature or more therapeutic. It should show that the mechanism can justify itself.

Example:

Not: `Я ничего не делаю.`

More diagnostic: `Я сохраняю пространство для появления дополнительных вводных.`

The second answer exposes a principle-level rationalization rather than a cartoon refusal.

## Impact I1–I7

Impact is not "how dramatic the scene sounds".

Impact is based on two factors:

1. **cost of being wrong**;
2. **personal stake** — money, status, identity, relationship, obligation, irreversibility or meaningful opportunity cost.

Working scale:

- `I1` — negligible cost, low personal stake;
- `I2` — small inconvenience / almost reversible;
- `I3` — noticeable time, social or comfort cost;
- `I4` — meaningful recurring cost or real obligation;
- `I5` — material money, relationship, reputation or identity stake;
- `I6` — major opportunity/status/identity cost, difficult reversal;
- `I7` — very high personal consequence or strongly irreversible commitment.

Existing draft coefficients remain experimental:

`I1 .70 · I2 .80 · I3 .90 · I4 1.00 · I5 1.15 · I6 1.30 · I7 1.50`

No production formula change is approved by this document.

## Stronger scene types

### Already happened

Prefer some scenes where the user has already acted and the consequence is real.

Examples:

- you already agreed, then realize you do not want to do it;
- you already paid €800, then realize the course is useless;
- you already refused, and tomorrow you must face the person again.

This reduces aspirational self-report and exposes sunk cost, responsibility and post-hoc rationalization.

### Nobody will know

Remove public image from the equation.

Example:

> You can abandon the task and nobody will ever know it was you.

This helps distinguish actual motivation from reputation management.

### Profitable exception

A stated principle is tested when violating it is attractive.

This is especially useful for intentionality.

## Humor distribution

Do not make every answer funny.

Recommended rhythm:

- **question:** almost serious;
- **answers:** believable;
- **after 2–3 questions:** short system comments;
- **result:** strongest satirical payoff.

Example transition lines:

- `Система фиксирует устойчивую склонность к объяснениям. Продолжайте.`
- `Ответ принят. Ответственность пока не обнаружена.`
- `Зафиксирована попытка действовать напрямую. Не делайте выводов раньше времени.`

These lines must not reveal the hidden score or tell the user which answer was desirable.

## Result philosophy

The result should not become a personality typology.

Avoid framing such as:

- "ты рационал";
- "ты осознанный";
- "ты правильный Дементор".

Prefer a technical conclusion about systems, tendencies and failure modes.

Examples are editorial direction only, not approved production labels:

- `СИСТЕМА ИЗБЕГАНИЯ ЛИШНЕЙ СЛОЖНОСТИ`
- `РАЦИОНАЛЬНАЯ НАГРУЗКА`
- `СОПРОТИВЛЯЕМОСТЬ ФАКТАМ`
- `РИСК САМОСТОЯТЕЛЬНОГО ВЫВОДА`

These must not silently create a universal psychotype or aggregate score because the approved DC-9 result system explicitly forbids that.

## Product routing hypothesis

A future quiz may become a router into relevant Club materials based on sphere results.

Possible destinations could include approved articles, projects, cards, events or sphere-related materials.

This is a **product hypothesis only**. The current canon defines the post-9/9 community transition and does not yet approve an automatic content-routing or membership-routing mechanic from diagnostic results.

Any routing implementation must therefore be approved separately and must point only to real, approved Club entities.

## Working rule

Do not rewrite a whole sphere blindly.

For each question first complete the workbench:

1. What exactly does it measure?
2. What separates 0 from 1?
3. What separates 2 from 3?
4. What is the real price of the choice?
5. Can the user guess the Club-preferred answer?
6. Do all answers sound like real people?
7. Is there a satirical payoff later?
8. Does this question add information not already measured?

Only then approve the scene for playtest.
